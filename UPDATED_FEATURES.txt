HRIS UPDATED FEATURES

1. Employee photo avatars are no longer circular; they use a small rounded rectangle.
2. Attendance report now shows Work Days, Records, Total Hours, Break Hours, Regular Hours, OT Hours, Late Minutes, and Special Working Days.
3. Payroll cutoff is enforced as:
   - 1 to 15
   - 16 to the last day of the month (30, 31, 28, or 29 as applicable)
4. Workday & Holiday Calendar added for HR/Admin:
   - Working Day
   - Special Working Day (2.00x default rule when actually worked)
   - Special Non-Working Day
   - Non-Working Day
5. Payroll automatically reads the calendar and adds the Special Working Day premium when an employee actually works the marked date.
6. Payslip now shows the Special Working Day premium separately.
7. Web payroll and API payroll use the same cutoff and Special Working Day rules.
8. Password policy for new/changed employee passwords: minimum 10 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character.
9. Demo accounts admin, superadmin, and msantos were upgraded from password123 only when that weak password is still in use.
